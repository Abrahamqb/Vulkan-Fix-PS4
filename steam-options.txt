============================================================================
 STEAM LAUNCH OPTIONS  (copy and paste)
============================================================================

--- 64-BIT GAMES (Unity, etc.) --------------------------------------------

FORM A (recommended: uses the universal launcher, respects %command%):

/path/to/repo/vulkan-run.sh --mesa 25.3.5 -- %command% -force-d3d11

FORM B (equivalent with source; Steam runs it with /bin/sh -> bash):

bash -c '. /path/to/repo/vulkan-env.sh --mesa 25.3.5; exec "$@"' _ %command% -force-d3d11


Variants:
  Mesa 26.0.4        -> change "--mesa 25.3.5" to "--mesa 26.0.4"
  With FPS counter   -> add --hud=fps   (Form A)  /  DXVK_HUD=fps before the command (Form B)
  Without -force-d3d11 -> remove it if the game is not Unity/D3D12


--- 32-BIT GAMES ----------------------------------------------------------

  PROTON_USE_WINED3D=1 %command%

  (There is no stable 32-bit RADV; OpenGL/wined3d works on this PS4.)


--- NON-STEAM GAMES ADDED TO STEAM ----------------------------------------

1. Steam > Add a Game > Add a Non-Steam Game > choose the .exe
2. Properties > Force the use of a specific Steam Play compatibility tool > Proton 8.0
3. Launch Options:

   /path/to/repo/vulkan-run.sh --mesa 25.3.5 -- %command%

   If the .exe is 32-bit:

   PROTON_USE_WINED3D=1 %command%


--- NON-STEAM GAMES WITHOUT STEAM (terminal) ------------------------------

64-bit:
  /path/to/repo/vulkan-run.sh --proton -- "/run/media/USER/External/Games/Example Game/game.exe"

32-bit:
  /path/to/repo/vulkan-run.sh --wined3d --proton -- "/run/media/USER/External/Games/Example Game/game32.exe"


--- CONFIRM THAT IT WORKS -------------------------------------------------

Add --hud=fps,devinfo (Form A) or DXVK_HUD=devinfo (Form B).
The top-left corner should show:  Mesa 25.3.5 (git-39f24bf409)  /  RADV LIVERPOOL
