## About the Project / Sobre el proyecto

### 🇺🇸 English

I won't hide the obvious: this project was developed on a large scale using artificial intelligence, always under my direct supervision. It all started with a series of simple tests and scripts that tackled the problem in a very simplified way, taking as a base and inspiration the solutions proposed by the [Noob404YT](https://github.com/noob404yt/ps4-custom-mesa-archlinux) channel and repository.

From those initial references, I shaped my own solution, which I then refined, structured, and expanded considerably with the help of AI. The final result of that process is what I have gathered and organized in this repository.

This project is completely open to the community: if you want to make modifications, contribute improvements, report bugs, or leave any kind of constructive feedback, all contributions are more than welcome!
